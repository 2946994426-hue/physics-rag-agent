# 1927 年诺贝尔物理学奖

**获奖者**：Arthur H. Compton、C.T.R. Wilson
**颁奖日期**：1927-11-10
**奖金**：4,544,453 SEK（调整后）

## 获奖理由

### Arthur H. Compton
全名：Arthur Holly Compton
份额：1/2
获奖理由：for his discovery of the effect named after him

### C.T.R. Wilson
全名：Charles Thomson Rees Wilson
份额：1/2
获奖理由：for his method of making the paths of electrically charged particles visible by condensation of vapour
