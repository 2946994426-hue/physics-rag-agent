# 2002 年诺贝尔物理学奖

**获奖者**：Raymond Davis Jr.、Masatoshi Koshiba、Riccardo Giacconi
**颁奖日期**：2002-10-08
**奖金**：15,218,228 SEK（调整后）

## 获奖理由

### Raymond Davis Jr.
份额：1/4
获奖理由：for pioneering contributions to astrophysics, in particular for the detection of cosmic neutrinos

### Masatoshi Koshiba
份额：1/4
获奖理由：for pioneering contributions to astrophysics, in particular for the detection of cosmic neutrinos

### Riccardo Giacconi
份额：1/2
获奖理由：for pioneering contributions to astrophysics, which have led to the discovery of cosmic X-ray sources
