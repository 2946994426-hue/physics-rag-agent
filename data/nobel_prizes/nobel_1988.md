# 1988 年诺贝尔物理学奖

**获奖者**：Leon M. Lederman、Melvin Schwartz、Jack Steinberger
**颁奖日期**：1988-10-19
**奖金**：5,874,628 SEK（调整后）

## 获奖理由

### Leon M. Lederman
份额：1/3
获奖理由：for the neutrino beam method and the demonstration of the doublet structure of the leptons through the discovery of the muon neutrino

### Melvin Schwartz
份额：1/3
获奖理由：for the neutrino beam method and the demonstration of the doublet structure of the leptons through the discovery of the muon neutrino

### Jack Steinberger
份额：1/3
获奖理由：for the neutrino beam method and the demonstration of the doublet structure of the leptons through the discovery of the muon neutrino
