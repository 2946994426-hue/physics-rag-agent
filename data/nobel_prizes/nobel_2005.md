# 2005 年诺贝尔物理学奖

**获奖者**：Roy J. Glauber、John L. Hall、Theodor W. Hänsch
**颁奖日期**：2005-10-04
**奖金**：14,809,494 SEK（调整后）

## 获奖理由

### Roy J. Glauber
份额：1/2
获奖理由：for his contribution to the quantum theory of optical coherence

### John L. Hall
份额：1/4
获奖理由：for their contributions to the development of laser-based precision spectroscopy, including the optical frequency comb technique

### Theodor W. Hänsch
份额：1/4
获奖理由：for their contributions to the development of laser-based precision spectroscopy, including the optical frequency comb technique
