# 2020 年诺贝尔物理学奖

**获奖者**：Roger Penrose、Reinhard Genzel、Andrea Ghez
**颁奖日期**：2020-10-06
**奖金**：12,361,835 SEK（调整后）

## 获奖理由

### Roger Penrose
份额：1/2
获奖理由：for the discovery that black hole formation is a robust prediction of the general theory of relativity

### Reinhard Genzel
份额：1/4
获奖理由：for the discovery of a supermassive compact object at the centre of our galaxy

### Andrea Ghez
份额：1/4
获奖理由：for the discovery of a supermassive compact object at the centre of our galaxy
