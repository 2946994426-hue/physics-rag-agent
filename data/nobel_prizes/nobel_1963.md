# 1963 年诺贝尔物理学奖

**获奖者**：Eugene Wigner、Maria Goeppert Mayer、J. Hans D. Jensen
**颁奖日期**：1963-11-05
**奖金**：3,590,371 SEK（调整后）

## 获奖理由

### Eugene Wigner
全名：Eugene Paul Wigner
份额：1/2
获奖理由：for his contributions to the theory of the atomic nucleus and the elementary particles, particularly through the discovery and application of fundamental symmetry principles

### Maria Goeppert Mayer
份额：1/4
获奖理由：for their discoveries concerning nuclear shell structure

### J. Hans D. Jensen
份额：1/4
获奖理由：for their discoveries concerning nuclear shell structure
