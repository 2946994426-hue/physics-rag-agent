# 1954 年诺贝尔物理学奖

**获奖者**：Max Born、Walther Bothe
**颁奖日期**：1954-11-03
**奖金**：3,338,644 SEK（调整后）

## 获奖理由

### Max Born
份额：1/2
获奖理由：for his fundamental research in quantum mechanics, especially for his statistical interpretation of the wavefunction

### Walther Bothe
份额：1/2
获奖理由：for the coincidence method and his discoveries made therewith
