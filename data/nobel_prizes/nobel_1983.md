# 1983 年诺贝尔物理学奖

**获奖者**：Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar、William A. Fowler
**颁奖日期**：1983-10-19
**奖金**：4,698,151 SEK（调整后）

## 获奖理由

### Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar
份额：1/2
获奖理由：for his theoretical studies of the physical processes of importance to the structure and evolution of the stars

### William A. Fowler
全名：William Alfred Fowler
份额：1/2
获奖理由：for his theoretical and experimental studies of the nuclear reactions of importance in the formation of the chemical elements in the universe
