# 1986 年诺贝尔物理学奖

**获奖者**：Ernst Ruska、Gerd Binnig、Heinrich Rohrer
**颁奖日期**：1986-10-15
**奖金**：5,182,514 SEK（调整后）

## 获奖理由

### Ernst Ruska
份额：1/2
获奖理由：for his fundamental work in electron optics, and for the design of the first electron microscope

### Gerd Binnig
份额：1/4
获奖理由：for their design of the scanning tunneling microscope

### Heinrich Rohrer
份额：1/4
获奖理由：for their design of the scanning tunneling microscope
