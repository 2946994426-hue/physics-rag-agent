# 1903 年诺贝尔物理学奖

**获奖者**：Henri Becquerel、Pierre Curie、Marie Curie
**颁奖日期**：1903-11-12
**奖金**：9,857,642 SEK（调整后）

## 获奖理由

### Henri Becquerel
全名：Antoine Henri Becquerel
份额：1/2
获奖理由：in recognition of the extraordinary services he has rendered by his discovery of spontaneous radioactivity

### Pierre Curie
份额：1/4
获奖理由：in recognition of the extraordinary services they have rendered by their joint researches on the radiation phenomena discovered by Professor Henri Becquerel

### Marie Curie
全名：Marie Curie, née Skłodowska
份额：1/4
获奖理由：in recognition of the extraordinary services they have rendered by their joint researches on the radiation phenomena discovered by Professor Henri Becquerel
