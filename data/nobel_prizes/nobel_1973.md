# 1973 年诺贝尔物理学奖

**获奖者**：Leo Esaki、Ivar Giaever、Brian D. Josephson
**颁奖日期**：1973-10-23
**奖金**：4,213,275 SEK（调整后）

## 获奖理由

### Leo Esaki
份额：1/4
获奖理由：for their experimental discoveries regarding tunneling phenomena in semiconductors and superconductors, respectively

### Ivar Giaever
份额：1/4
获奖理由：for their experimental discoveries regarding tunneling phenomena in semiconductors and superconductors, respectively

### Brian D. Josephson
全名：Brian David Josephson
份额：1/2
获奖理由：for his theoretical predictions of the properties of a supercurrent through a tunnel barrier, in particular those phenomena which are generally known as the Josephson effects
