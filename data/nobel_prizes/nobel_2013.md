# 2013 年诺贝尔物理学奖

**获奖者**：François Englert、Peter Higgs
**颁奖日期**：2013-10-08
**奖金**：10,578,918 SEK（调整后）

## 获奖理由

### François Englert
份额：1/2
获奖理由：for the theoretical discovery of a mechanism that contributes to our understanding of the origin of mass of subatomic particles, and which recently was confirmed through the discovery of the predicted fundamental particle, by the ATLAS and CMS experiments at CERN's Large Hadron Collider

### Peter Higgs
全名：Peter W. Higgs
份额：1/2
获奖理由：for the theoretical discovery of a mechanism that contributes to our understanding of the origin of mass of subatomic particles, and which recently was confirmed through the discovery of the predicted fundamental particle, by the ATLAS and CMS experiments at CERN's Large Hadron Collider
