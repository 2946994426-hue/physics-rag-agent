# 1979 年诺贝尔物理学奖

**获奖者**：Sheldon Glashow、Abdus Salam、Steven Weinberg
**颁奖日期**：1979-10-15
**奖金**：3,778,486 SEK（调整后）

## 获奖理由

### Sheldon Glashow
全名：Sheldon Lee Glashow
份额：1/3
获奖理由：for their contributions to the theory of the unified weak and electromagnetic interaction between elementary particles, including, inter alia, the prediction of the weak neutral current
