# 1974 年诺贝尔物理学奖

**获奖者**：Martin Ryle、Antony Hewish
**颁奖日期**：1974-10-15
**奖金**：4,126,741 SEK（调整后）

## 获奖理由

### Martin Ryle
全名：Sir Martin Ryle
份额：1/2
获奖理由：for their pioneering research in radio astrophysics: Ryle for his observations and inventions, in particular of the aperture synthesis technique, and Hewish for his decisive role in the discovery of pulsars

### Antony Hewish
份额：1/2
获奖理由：for their pioneering research in radio astrophysics: Ryle for his observations and inventions, in particular of the aperture synthesis technique, and Hewish for his decisive role in the discovery of pulsars
