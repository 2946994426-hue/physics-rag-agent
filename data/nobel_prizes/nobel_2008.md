# 2008 年诺贝尔物理学奖

**获奖者**：Yoichiro Nambu、Makoto Kobayashi、Toshihide Maskawa
**颁奖日期**：2008-10-07
**奖金**：13,817,016 SEK（调整后）

## 获奖理由

### Yoichiro Nambu
份额：1/2
获奖理由：for the discovery of the mechanism of spontaneous broken symmetry in subatomic physics

### Makoto Kobayashi
份额：1/4
获奖理由：for the discovery of the origin of the broken symmetry which predicts the existence of at least three families of quarks in nature

### Toshihide Maskawa
份额：1/4
获奖理由：for the discovery of the origin of the broken symmetry which predicts the existence of at least three families of quarks in nature
